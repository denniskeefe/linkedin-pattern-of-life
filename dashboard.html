Collection notes & caveats

Timestamps are exact. LinkedIn's activity IDs are snowflake identifiers — the high 41 bits are the publication time in milliseconds. Every time on this page is decoded from the ID itself and converted to the reporting timezone, so it survives the feed's vague relative labels.

Engagement is a snapshot, not a final score. Reaction and comment counts were read on the collection date. Recent posts have had days to accumulate; the oldest have had the length of the window. That alone biases the reach chart toward older posts.

This is broadcast activity, not total platform activity. The activity feed surfaces posts and reposts. Comments and reactions left on other people's posts are not counted here, so real time-on-platform is wider than these charts show.

Collection is near-complete, not provably complete. The public activity feed paginates non-deterministically — independent sweeps of the same range return overlapping but non-identical sets. Counts here are a floor, not a census.
