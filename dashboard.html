Collection notes & caveats

Timestamps are exact. LinkedIn's activity IDs are snowflake identifiers — the high 41 bits are the publication time in milliseconds. Every time on this page is decoded from the ID itself and converted to America/Chicago, so it survives the feed's vague relative labels.

Engagement is a snapshot, not a final score. Reaction and comment counts were read on 11 September 2026. Recent posts have had days to accumulate; the oldest have had a month. That alone biases the reach chart toward older posts.

This is broadcast activity, not total platform activity. The activity feed surfaced posts and reposts only. Comments and reactions left on other people's posts are not counted here, so the real "active on LinkedIn" hours are almost certainly wider than what these charts show.

Collection is near-complete, not provably complete. The public activity feed paginates non-deterministically — two independent sweeps of the same 31 days returned overlapping but non-identical sets, and this dataset is their union. A third sweep could surface a few more events. Treat counts as a floor.
