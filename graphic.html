Counts are a floor, not a census. The activity feed paginates non-deterministically: independent passes over the same range return overlapping but non-identical sets. Collection takes the union of several passes, so anything missed is missing from these figures too.

Times are decoded from each activity id — a snowflake whose high bits are the publication time in milliseconds — not from the feed's relative labels. Hours with fewer than three events are marked thin; they are anecdotes.
