Before you collect someone

LinkedIn's terms prohibit automated collection. The practical exposure is restriction of the account doing the collecting — yours.

Individual pattern-of-life needs a basis. These reports map when a named person is reliably reachable and, more usefully to the wrong reader, when they are not. Inside an authorized engagement that is a finding; outside one it is something else, and the data having been public does not change which.

Collected subjects stay local. subjects/ is gitignored and --publish refuses any subject not marked --self.
